Friend coach vs Hype & smack

Ranked is stressful enough. LOLCallout lets you pick the vibe of the voice in your ear without changing the underlying high-% coaching.

Friend coach

Calm, supportive duo partner. Direct facts, permission to fail, no mockery after deaths. Best when you want a serious climb session and clear next plays.

Example: “Ahri: 30% HP with 1400g — best move is base now.”

Hype & smack

Chaotic-good duo energy: hypes clean converts, lightly roasts ego plays, keeps the call first and the joke second. Still bans toxicity and never replaces advice with pure insults.

Example: “Two down — plates now. Free money, take it.”

How to switch

Open the LOLCallout app → Settings → Coach personality → Friend coach or Hype & smack. Density (Quiet / Normal / Talkative) is separate.
