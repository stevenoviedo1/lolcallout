Clean companion — not a cheat

LOLCallout is designed as a second-monitor AI coach for League of Legends. You still play every skillshot. The software never injects into the game client.

What “clean” means here

  • No DLL injection or memory writes into League
  • No automation of movement, spells, or shop
  • No fog hacks or enemy cooldown trackers beyond legal data
  • Live Client + optional screen capture you control
  • Advice only — you always control the champion

Why that matters for ranked

Climbing is about decisions under pressure. A coach that forces plays for you isn’t coaching — and it risks competitive integrity. LOLCallout aims for the opposite: high-% voice callouts and habit feedback so you get better.
