How the live AI League coach sees the board

LOLCallout is a Windows companion that coaches from legal Live Client data — the same class of stats a second monitor HUD can show — plus an AI layer that turns those facts into short voice callouts.

What the coach can know in real time

  • You: champion, level, KDA, CS, gold unspent, HP%, ability ranks, items
  • Scoreboard: every player’s level, deaths, alive/dead state, lane role when exposed
  • Man advantage: who is dead and for roughly how long (respawn windows)
  • Ult unlocked: enemies at level 6+ have ultimate unlocked (not “off cooldown”)
  • Threats: fed enemies and kit-aware respect lists (engage, assassins, etc.)
  • Events: kills, towers, dragons, barons as the client reports them

What it will never invent

Competitive integrity matters. The coach does not claim enemy ability cooldowns, fog-of-war positions, or unseen summoner spell timers. When it says “Malphite has ult unlocked”, that means level 6+ and alive — not a wallhack timer.

How callouts stay useful (not spam)

The coach scores insights — death, low HP, numbers swing, gold sit, ult-unlocked threats — and only speaks when the score clears a threshold. Recent lines are blocked from repeating so you don’t hear the same script every fight.

Two personalities

In settings you can pick Friend coach or Hype & smack. Facts stay the same; tone changes.
