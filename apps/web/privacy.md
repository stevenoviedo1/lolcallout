# LOLCallout Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Not legal advice. Have a lawyer review before charging customers.**

**Last updated:** 2026-10-18

## What we collect
- Account info (email) when you sign up
- Subscription/billing metadata (via payment processor; we do not store full card numbers)
- Session records (match snapshot stats) and post-game summaries saved to your account history (summaries are generated on your PC)
- Technical logs (errors, usage meters for fair use)

## What we do **not** do
- We do not inject into the League client or read game process memory
- Live game stats are read **locally** on your PC via Riot’s Live Client Data endpoint when you run the agent
- **AI coaching runs locally on your PC** (via Ollama). Your game data and coach prompts are **not sent to any AI provider**
- We do not capture or upload screenshots
- **Habit memory stays on your PC.** The agent keeps rolling stats from your recent games (deaths while ahead, deaths near objectives, CS/min, control wards bought, gold unspent at death, champion played) in `~/.lolcallout/profile.json` on your computer. It is used only for your local post-game summary, champ-select plan and the "My habits" panel. It is **never sent to our server**, and you can wipe it any time with **Reset habits** (or by deleting the file)

## How we use data
- Provide coaching, history, and voice features
- Enforce fair-use limits and prevent abuse
- Improve product quality

## Third parties
- No AI provider: callouts, chat and summaries are generated by a model running on your own PC
- Payment processors (e.g. Stripe) process payments
- Optional cloud voice (ElevenLabs), only if you select it: the text of a spoken line is sent to generate audio. The default browser voice stays on your PC
- Their policies apply to their processing

## Retention
- Match/session history retained while your account is active, or as required by law
- You may request deletion of account data by contacting support
